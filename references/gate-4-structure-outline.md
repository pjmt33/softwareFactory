## Questions

1. What are the phases, in order? Each phase must deliver one testable result.
2. For each phase: what validation proves it's done? (command to run, expected
   output, or manual check)
3. Is each phase vertical (end-to-end thin slice) or horizontal (layer by layer)?
   Horizontal phases should be split or merged into vertical ones.
